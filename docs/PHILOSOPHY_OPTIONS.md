# MoonSuite 产品哲学候选 / Product Philosophy Options

状态：最终选择已确认并同步到中英文产品数据。候选保留为决策记录。

最终选择：`Claw-B, Book-C, Town-B, Flow-C, Desk-A, Gate-A, Proj-C, Robo-C, Moon-A, Mold-A, Cast-C, Find-A`

- A：传播型——锋利、好记，适合首页。
- B：工程型——边界清楚，适合技术受众。
- C：综合型——兼顾传播与可信度，默认推荐。

## 01 · MoonClaw

- A｜执行，是智能体对世界作出的唯一承诺。<br>
  *Execution is the only promise an agent makes to the world.*
- B｜智能不是一次回答，而是能够留下证据、继续运行并接受纠偏的行动。<br>
  *Intelligence is not an answer; it is action that leaves evidence, keeps running, and accepts correction.*
- C（推荐）｜智能体不因会说话而成立，只因它能留下可验证的行动。<br>
  *An agent is not defined by speech, but by actions that can be verified.*

## 02 · MoonBook

- A｜记忆不是数据库，而是智能体与世界之间的三本账：信息差、认知差、执行差。<br>
  *Memory is not a database; it is three ledgers between an agent and the world: information, cognition, and execution gaps.*
- B｜记忆的价值不在保存，而在持续缩小信息差、认知差与执行差。<br>
  *Memory matters not because it stores, but because it keeps closing information, cognition, and execution gaps.*
- C（推荐）｜记忆不是过去的仓库，而是下一次行动的校正器。<br>
  *Memory is not a warehouse of the past; it is the calibration system for the next action.*

## 03 · Moontown

- A｜组织不是被设计出来的，而是在日程与路由之间自下而上涌现的。<br>
  *Organizations are not designed into existence; they emerge bottom-up between cadence and routing.*
- B｜组织不是被设计出来的图，而是角色、记忆与规则长期互动后涌现出的秩序。<br>
  *An organization is not a designed chart, but order emerging from the long interaction of roles, memory, and rules.*
- C（推荐）｜当角色拥有记忆、边界与节奏，组织就会从协作中长出来。<br>
  *Give roles memory, boundaries, and cadence, and organization grows out of collaboration.*

## 04 · MoonFlow

- A｜编排的真谛是敬畏失败：每一次执行都必须失败闭合、可恢复、可重放。<br>
  *Orchestration begins with respect for failure: every execution must fail closed, recover, and replay.*
- B｜复杂目标只有被拆成带证据、可重试、可回放的工作，才真正开始存在。<br>
  *A complex goal begins to exist only when it becomes evidenced, retryable, and replayable work.*
- C（推荐）｜真正的编排，不保证永不失败，而保证失败之后仍能继续。<br>
  *Real orchestration does not promise never to fail; it promises the work can continue afterward.*

## 05 · MoonDesk

- A｜最好的软件不是被写出来的，而是从一份可执行文档里长出来的。<br>
  *The best software is not merely written; it grows from an executable document.*
- B｜最好的智能体界面不是替人思考，而是让人看见、介入并授权机器的思考。<br>
  *The best agent interface does not think for people; it lets them see, intervene in, and authorize machine reasoning.*
- C（推荐）｜人负责表达意图与裁决结果，桌面负责让两者之间的一切可见。<br>
  *People express intent and judge outcomes; the desktop makes everything between them visible.*

## 06 · MoonGate

- A｜看见流量，才能看见智能的代价。<br>
  *Only by seeing the traffic can we see the cost of intelligence.*
- B｜模型能力只有穿过可观测、可路由、可降级的门，才会成为可靠基础设施。<br>
  *Model capability becomes reliable infrastructure only after it passes through observable, routable, degradable gates.*
- C（推荐）｜智能若不可观测、不可路由、不可降级，就还不是基础设施。<br>
  *If intelligence cannot be observed, routed, and degraded safely, it is not infrastructure yet.*

## 07 · MoonProj

- A｜ERP 不应该是人伺候系统，而是系统伺候人。<br>
  *People should not serve the ERP; the ERP should serve the people.*
- B｜智能体可以建议与执行，但组织的权力、账本与责任必须仍由组织本身拥有。<br>
  *Agents may advise and execute, but the organization must still own its authority, ledgers, and accountability.*
- C（推荐）｜公司的经营事实应从执行中自然沉淀，而不是靠人重复录入。<br>
  *A company’s operating truth should settle out of execution, not repeated human data entry.*

## 08 · MoonRobo

- A｜物理世界的每一次动作，都必须先赢得数字世界的授权。<br>
  *Every action in the physical world must first earn authorization in the digital world.*
- B｜机器人不是被智能体控制的肢体，而是必须用证据与安全边界协商行动的现实伙伴。<br>
  *A robot is not an agent-controlled limb, but a physical partner whose actions are negotiated through evidence and safety boundaries.*
- C（推荐）｜让智能拥有身体之前，先让每一个动作拥有证据与边界。<br>
  *Before intelligence gets a body, give every action evidence and boundaries.*

## 09 · MoonMoon

- A｜最远的外包，是把工厂开到月球上去。<br>
  *The furthest outsourcing is opening the factory on the Moon.*
- B｜在最遥远的环境里，自主的前提不是勇敢，而是证据、能量与可逆决策。<br>
  *In the most remote environment, autonomy begins not with courage but with evidence, energy, and reversible decisions.*
- C（推荐）｜当自主系统能在月面保持诚实，地球上的业务就不再遥远。<br>
  *When an autonomous system can remain honest on the Moon, no business on Earth is truly remote.*

## 10 · MoonMold

- A｜展示用的漂亮模型，永远不能冒充工程真理。<br>
  *A beautiful presentation model must never impersonate engineering truth.*
- B｜设计的可信度来自每次表示转换都知道自己保留了什么、丢失了什么。<br>
  *Design earns trust when every representation change knows what it preserved and what it lost.*
- C（推荐）｜每个模型都必须携带它保留的真相，以及它丢失的真相。<br>
  *Every model must carry both the truth it preserves and the truth it loses.*

## 11 · MoonCast

- A｜生成不是创作；通过八道证据门的生成，才是。<br>
  *Generation is not creation; generation that passes eight evidence gates is.*
- B｜内容生成不是灵感喷发，而是版权、预算、镜头、质量与交付共同约束的生产系统。<br>
  *Content generation is not a burst of inspiration, but a production system constrained by rights, budget, shots, quality, and delivery.*
- C（推荐）｜创作可以自由，生产必须让每一帧都有来处。<br>
  *Creation may be free; production must give every frame a provenance.*

## 12 · MoonFind

- A｜研究的尽头不是答案，而是下一个更好的问题。<br>
  *Research ends not with an answer, but with a better next question.*
- B｜研究不是找到答案，而是不断重构问题与证据之间的最短可信路径。<br>
  *Research is not finding an answer; it is repeatedly rebuilding the shortest trustworthy path between questions and evidence.*
- C（推荐）｜好研究不终结问题，而是持续缩短问题与证据之间的距离。<br>
  *Good research does not end the question; it keeps shortening the distance between question and evidence.*
